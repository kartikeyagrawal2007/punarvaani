**Research team:** _names, institution, contact phone and email_

## What we are asking
We will record a video of your face (mainly your mouth) while you read short Hindi sentences, first aloud and then silently. A session takes about 30–45 minutes, with breaks. If you agree, we may also use old voice recordings of you that you choose to share.

## Why
To build and test a system that can help people who have lost their voice speak again in their own voice.

## Your data
- Recordings are stored on encrypted, access-controlled storage used only by the research team. They are **never** uploaded to public sites or to the code repository.
- Videos are cropped to the mouth region for training. Full-face videos are deleted within _N_ months unless you allow longer storage below.
- In publications we report only aggregate results. Your face or voice appears in a demo **only** if you tick the box below.
- You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. If you do, your recordings are deleted and removed from future model training.

## Risks and benefits
There are no known risks beyond mild tiredness. There is no direct benefit to you. You will receive _compensation, if any_.
